# Knowledge base excerpts

## Excerpt 1 (source: returns-policy.md, score 0.93)

Returns policy, section 3. Customers can return unused products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Products must be in their original packaging. Garden furniture over 20 kg is collected from the customer's address by our courier, and the collection is free of charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned product passes inspection. Plants and seeds cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged, in which case the customer must send a photo within 48 hours of delivery.
